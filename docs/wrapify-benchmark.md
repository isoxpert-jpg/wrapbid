# Wrapify benchmark for Wrapbid

Reviewed 6 September 2026.

Reference demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pdv68csBykw — **Wrapify Ad Dash 2.0 Demo** (3:41, published approximately 2020).

Current official product references:

## What to benchmark

Wrapify presents vehicle advertising as measurable media rather than merely a driver marketplace. Its advertiser dashboard centers on impressions, mileage, heat maps, daily routes, attribution, and retargeting. Its driver proposition is simple onboarding followed by campaign offers, with familiar 180/270/360 coverage packages.

Wrapbid should benchmark:

1. A campaign-first advertiser dashboard with reach, distance, vehicles, geography, and delivery status.
2. Clear visual previews of the purchased vehicle surface.
3. Simple driver onboarding and explicit campaign/installation states.
4. Evidence-based brand safety and recurring proof of performance.
5. Separation between planning metrics, engagement metrics, and payment eligibility.

## Where Wrapbid should deliberately differ

- **No continuous GPS in v1.** Show named commute zones and estimates; offer limited sampled-route verification later.
- **No exposed exact routes or home coordinates.** Pakistan and Afghanistan require a privacy-first trust model.
- **Self-serve and price-transparent.** Wrapify remains quote-led; Wrapbid's auction and visible panel pricing are its differentiation.
- **Budget-car and panel-level inventory.** Prioritize Alto, Mehran, Mira, Corolla, and Vitz rather than only full/partial premium wraps.
- **Proof before payout.** Vehicle identity, fresh challenge evidence, recurring checks, and fraud review control eligibility; QR scans do not.
- **Local-market operations.** PKR/AFN settlement, local printing, permits, and multilingual interfaces must be validated locally rather than copied from a US benchmark.
